# HELPORA Feature Roadmap

## Goal
Target: 500 product capabilities delivered in planned phases, not 500 fake placeholders.

## Phase 1 — Core app
1. Onboarding
2. Language preference
3. Home
4. Discover
5. Help requests
6. Categories
7. Local request persistence
8. Resolve requests
9. Delete requests
10. Profile
11. Trust Center
12. Settings
13. Inbox shell
14. Search
15. Saved items foundation
16. HP points foundation
17. Activity foundation
18. Privacy controls foundation
19. Community guidelines
20. Version information

## Phase 2 — Account and community
Authentication, registration, password recovery, profiles, avatars, reputation, verification, following, blocking, reporting, moderation, organizations, events, volunteering and notifications.

## Phase 3 — Help network
Location-aware discovery, maps, matching, urgency levels, availability, offers, requests, groups, organizations, multilingual assistance and accessibility.

## Phase 4 — Communication
Secure conversations, request chat, media sharing, translation assistance, notifications, message controls and moderation.

## Phase 5 — Trust and safety
Verification workflows, reporting, blocking, safety guidance, moderation queue, audit records, privacy settings and consent.

## Phase 6 — Growth and analytics
Privacy-respecting analytics, experiments, referrals, onboarding metrics, retention insights and product dashboards.
